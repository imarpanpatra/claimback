Your flight was late.
The airline owes you.

Claimback checks what really happened to your flight, reads the law that covers it, works out what you're owed, and fills in the airline's claim form. It stops before sending anything, so the final click is yours.

Live runs spend real Anakin credits, so they need an access code. The recording is a real run, replayed.

To run a claim live, enter a flight from the last 14 days and the access code.

    Read

    Live facts, two sources

    Gate times from FlightAware through Anakin's URL Scraper, cross-checked with AirHelp flight status through Anakin Wire. Then the official EU or UK regulation text, read live.

    Reason

    Decisions you can check

    The model works out which laws apply and what's owed, quoting the regulation. Every quote is checked against the page it came from, and every amount against a built-in table.

    Act

    The claim, filled in

    It finds the airline's claim form with Anakin Search and fills it in Anakin's recorded cloud browser, with form submission switched off so nothing can be sent. It also writes a claim letter you can send.
